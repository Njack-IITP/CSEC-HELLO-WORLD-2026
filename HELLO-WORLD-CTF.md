# Hello World CTF

## What Is This?

A week-long, beginner-friendly Capture The Flag (CTF) competition built into the walkthrough. As you work through each day's material, you'll encounter challenges with hidden flags to find. No prior CTF or security experience needed. The walkthrough teaches you everything you need to solve them.

## How It Works

- Each day's walkthrough contains **mini-challenges** with flags embedded in them
- Challenges build on what you just learned. They test understanding, not guesswork
- Submit flags on the CTFd scoreboard (see [SUBMISSION-GUIDE.md](./SUBMISSION-GUIDE.md))
- Your score accumulates on a live leaderboard throughout the week

## The Offline Event

At the end of the walkthrough week, there will be a **live, in-person CTF event** — a capstone that ties together everything from the week.

### What to Expect

- A guided, multi-stage challenge connecting recon, web exploitation, cryptography, and forensics
- Work individually or in pairs
- Hints available at every stage. The goal is everyone finishing, not gatekeeping
- Duration: ~2-3 hours

### Qualification

To qualify for the offline event, **complete all days of the walkthrough**. No minimum score, no trick. If you put in the work during the week, you're in.
