# Submission Guide

## Where Challenge Files Come From

When a day says "you'll be given a `.pcap`" (or a binary, a hex string, an exhibit), that file is an **attachment on the challenge itself on the CTFd scoreboard** — open the challenge, download the file from there. The link to CTFd and any files too big to attach are posted in the CSEC Discord. Nothing is committed to this roadmap repo; the walkthroughs teach the technique, CTFd hands you the material.

## How to Submit Flags

Flags look like this: `NJACK{some_text_here}`

When you find a flag during a challenge or mini-challenge:

1. Go to the CTFd scoreboard — check the CSEC Discord server for the link and login details
2. Find the challenge by name
3. Paste the **entire flag** including the `NJACK{` prefix and closing `}`
4. Hit submit — you'll get instant feedback (correct or wrong)

For all announcements, hints, help, and self-graded submissions, check the CSEC Discord server.

## Rules

- **No sharing flags** — finding it yourself is the point. Discuss approaches, not answers
- **Screenshots are required** for some challenges — the day's instructions will say when
- Some challenges are **self-graded** (worksheets, no single flag) — these are handled on Discord for completion credit, not on CTFd

## Points & Leaderboard

| Difficulty | Points |
|---|---|
| Easy | 10 |
| Medium | 20-25 |
| Capstone | 50 |

- The leaderboard updates live on CTFd as you submit correct flags
- Self-graded questions earn completion credit separately

## Prizes

Top performers on the leaderboard earn prizes. Details will be announced on Discord.

## Stuck?

- Re-read the day's walkthrough. The answer is always reachable from the material covered
- Ask in the CSEC Discord. Mentors are there to nudge you in the right direction
- Hints are available for most challenges after a cooldown period
